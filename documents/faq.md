# Learning Assistant – Frequently Asked Questions

*Sample document for the LMS Skills Intelligence Engine. Fictional content for demonstration only.*

## How are my skill gaps calculated?

For every skill a job role needs, the LMS compares your current level with the required level on the 1–5 proficiency scale. The gap is the required level minus your current level, or zero if you already meet it. A skill you do not have counts as level 0. The gap score summarises all gaps: 0 means you are ready for the role.

## What is the match percentage?

The match percentage shows how much of a role's requirements you already meet. Being above the required level in one skill does not make up for a gap in another skill.

## Why was a course recommended to me?

Each recommendation shows its reason, for example: "Your SQL is level 2 and Data Engineer needs level 4; this course takes it from level 2 to 4." The score combines four parts: how much of your gap the course closes, whether it starts at your level, whether its prerequisites are met, and how likely you are to complete it.

## What is a learning path?

A learning path is the list of courses that closes your gaps for a target role, in an order you can follow. Prerequisites always come first, and courses for mandatory skills come before optional ones. The path also shows your progress and the next action.

## Which role is my learning path for?

Your recorded career goal. If you have no career goal, the path uses your current role. You can record a career goal in the LMS (see the Learning and Development Policy).

## Do I need to take a prerequisite course if I already have the skill?

No. If your assessed skill level already covers what the prerequisite course teaches, the prerequisite counts as met.

## My skill level looks out of date. What should I do?

Completing a course does not update your skill record automatically. Ask your manager for a re-assessment after completing a course.

## Can I take a course again after dropping it?

Yes. You can re-enrol in a dropped course at any time.

## How many learning hours do I have?

40 hours of paid learning time per calendar year, in addition to mandatory compliance training.

## Is my data sent to an external AI service?

No. The assistant uses a language model that runs on company systems. Answers are based only on LMS data and approved documents, and each answer lists its sources.
