# Learning and Development Policy

*Sample document for the LMS Skills Intelligence Engine. The company and every rule below are fictional and exist only to demonstrate the AI assistant.*

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how employees use the Learning Management System (LMS) to build the skills their current role and their next role need. Learning is planned around skill gaps: the difference between an employee's current proficiency and the level a job role requires.

## 2. Learning time

- Every employee has **40 hours of paid learning time per calendar year**, in addition to mandatory compliance training.
- Learning time can be used for any active course in the LMS catalogue that closes a skill gap for the employee's current role or recorded career goal.
- Unused learning hours do not carry over to the next year.
- Courses longer than 20 hours should be spread over several weeks, agreed with the manager.

## 3. Enrolment and approval

- Employees enrol themselves in courses that close a gap for their **current role**; no approval is needed.
- Courses for a **career goal** (a different target role) need the manager's approval in the LMS before enrolment.
- Prerequisites must be completed first, unless the employee's assessed skill level already covers what the prerequisite course teaches.
- The LMS recommends courses and builds a learning path; employees and managers stay responsible for the final choice.

## 4. Completing courses

- A course is **Completed** when the final assessment is passed. The score is recorded in the training history.
- A course can be attempted **up to 5 times**. After the fifth attempt, the employee and manager agree a different course or a mentoring plan.
- A course with no activity for 90 days is marked **Dropped**. Employees can re-enrol in a dropped course at any time; the earlier attempt stays in the history.
- Completing a course does not change the skill record automatically. Skill levels are updated by a self-assessment, a manager assessment or a formal assessment.

## 5. Skill assessments

- Employees review their skill levels at least **once a year**, and after completing a course that raises a skill.
- A manager assessment or a formal assessment takes priority over a self-assessment.
- If an employee has completed courses at a higher level than their recorded skill level, they should ask their manager for a re-assessment.

## 6. Career goals

- Each employee may record **one active career goal** (a target job role) in the LMS.
- The career goal is used by the skills analysis, the training recommendations and the learning path.
- A goal can be changed at any time; the previous goal is marked **Withdrawn**, or **Achieved** when the employee moves into the role.

## 7. Certifications

External certifications (for example cloud certifications) are covered by the separate Certification Reimbursement Policy.

## 8. Privacy

Skill levels, career goals and training history are visible only to the employee, their manager and the Learning and Development team. The AI assistant runs on company systems and does not send employee data to external services.
