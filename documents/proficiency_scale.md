# Skill Proficiency Scale

*Sample document for the LMS Skills Intelligence Engine. Fictional content for demonstration only.*

All skills in the LMS are measured on the same five-level scale. Job roles state the level they require; employees record the level they have.

| Level | Label | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Beginner | Knows the basic ideas; needs step-by-step guidance. |
| 2 | Elementary | Completes simple tasks with some help. |
| 3 | Intermediate | Works independently on typical tasks. |
| 4 | Advanced | Handles complex tasks and guides others. |
| 5 | Expert | Recognised authority; sets standards and solves novel problems. |

## How levels are used

- A **required level** is the minimum a job role needs. A gap exists when the current level is below it.
- A skill an employee does not have is treated as **level 0**.
- Courses state the level they **start from** (the expected entry level) and the level they **bring you to**. A course that starts two or more levels above your current level is usually too hard: completion rates drop sharply in that case.
- Mandatory skills must be met for a role. Optional skills improve the match but are not required.
